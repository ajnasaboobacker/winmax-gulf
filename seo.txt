Step 1 — Fix crawlability and rendering first

Goal: make sure Google, Bing, and AI search systems can actually read the page content from the HTML or reliably rendered DOM.

Implement:

Convert the homepage, /pdlc, and all major service pages to SSR or SSG.
Ensure the main page content is present in rendered HTML:
page title
H1
intro paragraph
service copy
FAQs
internal links
contact/business info
Do not rely on client-side-only rendering for critical text.
Ensure all important links are real <a href=""> links, not click handlers or router-only pseudo-links.
Ensure important images are real <img> or <picture> elements, not CSS background images for primary discoverable assets.

Google says JavaScript pages go through crawling, rendering, and indexing, and Google can only reliably discover links if they are proper anchor tags with href. Google image indexing also works best with standard HTML image elements.

Acceptance criteria:

“View Source” or rendered HTML must contain meaningful body text for homepage and /pdlc.
Internal navigation links must appear as crawlable anchors.
Main product/service images must be indexable HTML images.

Goal: stop blocking resources needed for rendering and AI/search discovery.

Implement:

Keep blocking only genuinely private or useless areas, such as:

Recommended robots.txt baseline:

Disallow: /admin/

User-agent: OAI-SearchBot

Google says AI-feature eligibility still depends on normal Search access, and it specifically calls out that crawling must be allowed. OpenAI says sites that opt out of OAI-SearchBot will not be shown in ChatGPT search answers, and recommends allowing it in robots.txt.

Acceptance criteria:

No render-critical assets blocked by robots.
robots.txt publicly reachable.
OAI-SearchBot explicitly allowed.
